PID tuning

Kilns behave differently hot and cold: radiation makes losses much larger at high temperature. The controller keeps a few tuning points; between them it interpolates the gains by setpoint, and it adds each point's holding power (the power needed to stay at that temperature) as a baseline, so the PID only corrects around it. Measure points with step tests, typically one low (≈150 °C) and one high (≈750 °C).
